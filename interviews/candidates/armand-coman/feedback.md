# Armand Coman – Senior Java Developer

## Attitude

Armand demonstrated a positive, professional, and highly collaborative attitude throughout the interview. He appears to be a hands-on engineer who enjoys understanding systems deeply and solving practical business problems. His responses reflected ownership of the solutions he has delivered, particularly around integrations, large-scale data handling, and automation initiatives. He also showed willingness to mentor others and contribute beyond pure feature development.

## Communication

Communication was one of his strongest qualities. He consistently answered questions in a structured, detailed, and easy-to-follow manner, making the interview flow naturally. He was particularly effective when describing system architectures, technical decisions, and project challenges, connecting business requirements with implementation details. His English level is fully suitable for senior-level collaboration in international environments.

## Leadership Skills

While he does not appear to operate as a formal technical lead, he demonstrated several senior-level leadership characteristics. He described mentoring junior engineers, leading technical initiatives, and taking ownership of important quality improvements such as the Salesforce regression testing framework. His profile aligns well with a senior individual contributor who can positively influence engineering practices within a team.

## Technical Strengths

Strong backend engineering profile with solid expertise in Java, Spring Boot, Kotlin, Micronaut, distributed systems, API integrations, and database technologies. He demonstrated good understanding of JVM fundamentals, collections, functional programming concepts, dependency injection, JPA/Hibernate, and Git workflows. Particularly positive was his ability to explain real-world architectural decisions and discuss challenges around large datasets, microservices communication, GraphQL integrations, and automated testing. His experience across REST, GraphQL, Kafka, gRPC, MongoDB, and relational databases provides a well-rounded backend foundation.

## Areas for Improvement

The main improvement areas are around concurrency depth and operational experience. While he demonstrated good understanding of synchronization and race condition concepts, some concurrency topics such as deadlocks, livelocks, and atomic classes lacked the same level of confidence shown in other areas. Observability and production troubleshooting experience also appeared less developed compared to his application development experience. Additionally, his use of AI tooling is relatively limited compared to many senior engineers currently adopting AI-assisted workflows.

## Feedback Notes / Side Notes

One of the strongest signals during the interview was his ability to explain systems from both a business and technical perspective. Rather than jumping directly into technologies, he often started by describing the problem being solved and then explained the architecture and implementation choices. His Salesforce regression testing initiative was a particularly good example of ownership and practical engineering impact. Overall, he comes across as an experienced, hands-on backend engineer with solid real-world delivery experience.

## Overall Rating

**8/10**

**Recommendation to move forward for a Senior Java Backend Engineer role.** Armand demonstrates strong backend development skills, good communication, and practical experience designing and maintaining distributed systems. While there are opportunities to deepen some concurrency and observability topics, his overall technical foundation and engineering maturity align well with senior-level expectations.
